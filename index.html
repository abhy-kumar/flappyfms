Flappy Eagle – Free Online Arcade Browser Game

Flappy Eagle is a free online arcade browser game. Guide a soaring red eagle through neon-lit obstacles across three exciting game modes. No download or sign-up required — play instantly in any modern web browser on desktop or mobile.

How to Play Flappy Eagle

Press Spacebar, click, or tap the screen to make the eagle flap its wings and gain altitude. Gravity pulls it down — time your taps to navigate through the gaps between neon pillars. Each pillar you clear earns one point. Collect power-ups floating in the gaps for special bonuses. Hit a pillar or the ground and your flight ends.

Game Controls

Spacebar or Up Arrow key on desktop. Click or tap on mobile and tablet devices.

Game Modes

Classic Mode

The original Flappy Eagle experience. Standard gravity and pipe speed that gradually increases. Perfect for beginners learning the arcade mechanics.

Hardcore Mode

Fast-paced challenge with moving pillars and tighter gaps. Designed for experienced players seeking a serious arcade challenge.

Zen Mode

Relaxed gameplay with wider gaps and gentle gravity. Great for casual play or improving your timing and high score.

Power-Ups & Collectibles

🌟 Golden Feather

Collect the Golden Feather power-up to instantly earn +5 bonus points.

🛡️ Shield Orb

The Shield Orb protects you from a single pillar collision, giving you one extra life.

⏳ Slow-Mo Clock

Activating the Slow-Mo Clock reduces game speed and gravity for 6 seconds.

Features

  • Three game modes: Classic, Hardcore, and Zen
  • Three collectible power-ups with unique effects
  • Dynamic sky backgrounds that evolve as your score increases
  • High score tracking saved locally in your browser
  • Synthesized sound effects using the Web Audio API — no downloads
  • Fully responsive — plays on desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile
  • Achievement milestones at score thresholds
  • Instant play — no account, no download, no installation needed

About

Flappy Eagle (also known as Flappy FMS) is built with pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript. The game uses the Canvas API for 60 FPS rendering and the Web Audio API for zero-latency synthesized sound. Created by Abhishek Kumar.
