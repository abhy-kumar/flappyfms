Flappy FMS – Free Online Arcade Browser Game

Flappy FMS (formerly Flappy Eagle) is a free online arcade browser game. Guide a soaring red eagle through neon-lit obstacles across three game modes. No download or sign-up required — play instantly in any modern web browser on desktop or mobile.

How to Play Flappy FMS

Press Spacebar, Up Arrow, W, click, or tap the screen to make the eagle flap its wings and gain altitude. Gravity pulls it down — time your taps to navigate through the gaps between neon pillars. Each pillar you clear earns points, and clearing pillars back to back builds a combo multiplier worth up to 5× per pillar. Thread a gap tightly for a bonus "close call". Collect power-ups floating in the gaps. Hit a pillar or the ground and your flight ends.

Game Controls

Spacebar, Up Arrow or W on desktop. Click or tap on mobile and tablet devices. Press Escape or P to pause; M mutes the sound.

Game Modes

Classic Mode

The original Flappy FMS experience. Standard gravity, with pillar speed and gap width tightening gradually as your score climbs. Perfect for learning the arcade mechanics.

Hardcore Mode

Fast-paced challenge with vertically moving pillars and tighter gaps that shrink faster. Designed for experienced players seeking a serious arcade challenge.

Zen Mode

Relaxed gameplay with wider gaps, gentle gravity and no difficulty ramp at all. Great for casual play or improving your timing and high score.

Power-Ups & Collectibles

Golden Feather

Collect the Golden Feather power-up to instantly earn +5 bonus points.

Shield Orb

The Shield Orb protects you from a single pillar collision, giving you one extra life.

Slow-Mo Clock

Activating the Slow-Mo Clock reduces game speed and gravity for 6 seconds.

Magnet

The Magnet pulls nearby power-ups toward your eagle for 8 seconds.

2× Points

Doubles every point you score for 10 seconds, stacking with your combo multiplier.

Ghost

Phase straight through pillars without crashing for 5 seconds.

Features

  • Three game modes: Classic, Hardcore and Zen, each with its own high score
  • Six collectible power-ups with unique effects and on-screen timers
  • Combo multipliers up to 5× and bonus points for close calls
  • Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum medals awarded per run
  • 17 unlockable achievements and lifetime flight statistics
  • Dynamic sky that shifts from night blue to crimson dusk to cyber purple as you score
  • Synthesized sound effects using the Web Audio API — no audio downloads
  • Fully responsive — plays on desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile
  • Pause any time, with automatic pause when you switch tabs
  • Reduced-motion, screen-shake and haptics settings for comfort and accessibility
  • Instant play — no account, no download, no installation needed

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Flappy FMS free?

Yes — completely free, with no ads, sign-up or installation.

Are my high scores saved?

Yes. Each mode keeps its own high score in your browser's local storage, alongside your achievements and lifetime stats. Nothing is sent to a server.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The canvas scales to any screen and the controls are tap-based on touch devices.

About

Flappy FMS (formerly Flappy Eagle) is built with pure HTML5, CSS3 and Vanilla JavaScript. The game uses the Canvas API with a fixed 60 Hz simulation for consistent physics on any display, and the Web Audio API for zero-latency synthesized sound. Created by Abhishek Kumar.
